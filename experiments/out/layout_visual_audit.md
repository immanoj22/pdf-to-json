## Major implications

- Raw layout boxes are not a column/paragraph solution. PP fails two observed native region sequences: mixed main columns in column_26, and reversed text within one illustrated card. PP has usable scoped order on the other seven fixtures, with the limitations above. This is a nine-fixture observation, not a production accuracy estimate.
- The geometry method uses page-global gutters. Matching `2` on background_worksheet or complex_sidebar does **not** make page-global all-left-then-all-right sorting correct: it would take lower-left material before finishing the upper-right section. Section bands must precede within-band column order. Matching `1` on colored_box leaves marginal-sidebar scope unresolved.
- Geometry misses the lower two-column map body and upper two-column card body (reports1); it also interprets figure/caption space as a second prose column on both science fixtures (reports2 vs actual1). These are semantic scope mistakes even where x separation exists.
- Composite figure grouping remains necessary. On science_dissolving, both models detect the three panels separately. Model image boxes alone do not encode the user requirement many sub-images/labels = one figure.
- Neither pass column counts nor native region order proves whole paragraphs. In particular, column_100 and complex_sidebar have valid cross-column prose split across separate model regions. Root's token-preservation extraction must remain independent of omitted/mislabelled layout regions.
